About

Thinking ahead, building to last.

I work where strategy meets execution: finding the pattern in a complex problem, forming a view of where an organization could be in a few years, and turning it into steps that teams can adopt and own.

I have worked across data platforms, machine learning, and AI, from hands-on engineering to AI strategy and roadmaps. That range lets me connect roadmap decisions to what is technically feasible.

  • AnticipateTest emerging capabilities early and form a view on where they create value.
  • SynthesizeDistill complexity into clear frameworks, options, and decisions.
  • ConnectAlign business and engineering, and lead the change management that makes AI adoption stick.
  • LearnTurn each initiative into reusable insight.
